# Content Analyzer

Use this skill after the underlying source content has been retrieved.

Follow the detailed analysis workflow in [skill.md](./skill.md).

Critical contract:

- Distinguish source facts, author claims, and your interpretation.
- Do not analyze a search snippet as if it were the original source.
- Surface missing evidence and counterarguments instead of force-filling sections.
